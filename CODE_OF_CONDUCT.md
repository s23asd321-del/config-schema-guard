# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, constructive participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Assume good intent while staying precise.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Avoid harassment, insults, threats, and discriminatory language.
- Do not pressure people to share private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Publishing private information without permission.
- Posting real secrets, private configuration files, or private logs.
- Harassment or repeated hostile behavior.
- Encouraging illegal, abusive, or harmful use.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may limit participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.
